# Project Vision - SmartEats

## Why SmartEats?
Modern food delivery giants like Swiggy, Zomato, and Uber Eats operate on reactive architectures. They handle transaction processing (ordering, routing, payments) excellently but provide limited real-time predictive support for participating restaurants and drivers. As a result:
- Restaurants struggle with food waste due to over-preparation or under-preparedness during peak surges.
- Delivery partners face inefficient routing and unpredictable wait times.
- Surplus food is discarded rather than dynamically discounted or redirected to communities in need.

**SmartEats** is designed to shift food delivery from a purely transaction-oriented service to a **sustainability-first predictive ecosystem**. By combining a microservices backend with a machine learning-based **Restaurant Decision Support System (RDSS)**, SmartEats bridges the gap between customer convenience, restaurant operations, and environmental responsibility.

## Core Pillars
1. **Predictive Intelligence**: Leverage AI for demand forecasting and inventory tracking to minimize restaurant overhead.
2. **Eco-Friendly Sustainability**: Build automated mechanisms to flag food waste risks, offering dynamic discounts or pushing items to local NGOs for donation.
3. **Optimized Delivery**: A smarter routing/dispatching layer factoring in preparation times and real-time traffic to guarantee efficiency.
4. **Cloud-Native Scalability**: Maintain modular service boundaries designed to scale dynamically using modern containerization technologies.
