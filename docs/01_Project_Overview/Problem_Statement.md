# Problem Statement - SmartEats

## What problem are we solving?
1. **Inefficient Inventory Management & Food Wastage**:
   Restaurants lack actionable intelligence on how much food they will sell on any given day. Factors such as weather, days of the week, holidays, and localized events heavily influence order volume. Without predictive analytics, restaurants routinely over-prepare ingredients, leading to substantial food wastage at the end of the operating day.

2. **Reactive Dispatching & Routing**:
   Traditional platforms dispatch delivery drivers based purely on distance or availability, ignoring food preparation status. This results in drivers waiting long periods at restaurants, which burns fuel, increases delivery lateness, and lowers customer satisfaction.

3. **Inability to Redirect Surplus Food**:
   There is currently no seamless link between active inventory tracking, dynamic discounting for end-of-day surplus stock, and direct integration with local Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) to donate clean, consumable surplus food before it expires.

4. **Monolithic Architecture Limitations**:
   Traditional monolithic platforms struggle to scale parts of the system independently (such as spike loads in the cart or payment gateway during meal surges) and face challenges with single points of failure.
